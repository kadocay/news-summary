World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

1. Russia and Ukraine held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, their first direct talks in nine months; day one, about four hours, was called "constructive", prisoner swaps discussed, no deal, and a reported swap deal unconfirmed (AP, BBC).
2. World leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, where nearly 200 countries debate lifting electricity to 35% of global energy use by 2035; the US is absent for a second year (Reuters, UNFCCC).
3. The US House voted 211-204 to send eight impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, halting it for now; Rep. Thomas Massie was the only Republican dissenter (Reuters).
4. US prosecutors charged three men over an alleged Iran-backed plot against RAF Fairford air base in Britain; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved onto a military base after Iranian threats (NYT); France blamed Iran's Revolutionary Guard for hacking about 700 supermarket fridges, which Iran denies (BBC, NYT, AFP).
5. In the Strait of Hormuz, ADNOC said one of its vessels was damaged in an attack in UAE waters and Britain's UKMTO reported a tanker struck off Oman; no attacker was identified (Reuters).
6. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts, hardening bets on a December Fed hike; Asian shares rose — Nikkei up 0.8%, Kospi up 1.6% — as TSMC's October revenue rose 59.7% year on year (BLS, AP).
7. Anthropic set a $70-$78 range for its Nasdaq IPO, seeking up to $64bn at up to $1.4tn, Google and Nvidia each buying $10bn in a $26bn private placement (SEC filing, CNBC).
8. Fog pile-ups on Bulgaria's Trakia motorway killed 12 and injured 46 in about 86 vehicles, with Bulgaria holding a day of national mourning; a bus hit 32 vehicles in Seongnam, South Korea, leaving nine seriously hurt, 48 with minor injuries (Reuters, AP).
9. Confirmed Ebola cases in DR Congo passed 11,000: 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, government data showed (Reuters).
