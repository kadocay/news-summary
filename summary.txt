World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

1. Russia and Ukraine ended US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi agreeing a prisoner swap of over 1,000 each side, their first direct talks in nine months; no deal on territory (AP, BBC).
2. At COP31 in Antalya, Guterres urged leaders to phase out fossil fuels as nearly 200 countries debate lifting electricity to 35% of energy by 2035; the US is absent a second year (Reuters, UN).
3. The US House voted 211-204 to send eight impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, halting it; Rep. Thomas Massie was the only Republican dissenter (Reuters).
4. US prosecutors charged three men over an alleged Iran-linked plot at RAF Fairford; France blamed Iran's Revolutionary Guard for hacking about 700 supermarket fridges, which Iran denies (NYT, BBC, AFP).
5. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, hardening bets on a December Fed hike; the S&P 500 fell 0.4% and the 10-year yield rose to 4.98% (BLS, AP).
6. Anthropic set a $70-$78 range for its Nasdaq IPO, seeking up to $64bn at up to $1.4tn, with Google and Nvidia each buying $10bn in a $26bn placement (SEC filing, CNBC).
7. House chairmen gave Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang until 11 November to accept a briefing on his affair with an alleged Chinese spy, or face a subpoena; Nvidia's board is investigating (ABC News).
8. Indonesia's Vice President Gibran resigned, his aide said, after court rulings that his diploma failed election rules; the palace had not confirmed it (Reuters).
9. Spain held its first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, backed by the CCOO and UGT unions, ahead of a snap election on 29 November (Reuters).
10. London's Privy Council unanimously struck down Trinidad and Tobago's colonial-era ban on same-sex intimacy, ending activist Jason Jones's case from 2017 (AP, UN).
11. Ebola cases in DR Congo passed 11,000 — 11,042 cases, 5,387 deaths; Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown (Reuters).
